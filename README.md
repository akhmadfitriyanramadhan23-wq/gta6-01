Solmar Bay — an offline top-down open-world crime game: a seaside city to explore on foot and by car, with traffic and pedestrians, cars to steal, a wanted level with police, three story missions, taxi fares and hidden stashes. The city is a 3200×2400 seeded map on a 400 px road grid with a beach and sea to the west. Steal cars, dodge traffic, earn cash from missions, fares and stashes, and stay out of the police's reach — wanted level 0–5, busted means losing $100. HUD with cash, wanted stars, objective and distance, minimap, speed and car health; title, paused and busted panels; a save in localStorage; synthesized WebAudio sounds; deterministic `?shot=start|play|end` scenes.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080.
